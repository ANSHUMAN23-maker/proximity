A few ground rules

Keep these in mind for a safer experience with Proximity.

Just two details for setup

We only ask for your Bank Name and SIM Operator. We do not ask your Bank IFSC code, any OTP, or UPI access

Keep sensitive details private

Any kind of banking password or UPI pin is being entered on official government portals. Proximity never read or stores such information.

Check before you pay

Confirm the recipient's name and mobile number or QR details before sending a payment.
